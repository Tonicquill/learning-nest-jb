Our Story

Over a decade in education. A school built on the belief that every child deserves to be known.

Mission

To empower every child through personalised education in a nurturing community

We believe quality education is not about how many students you can fit in a classroom. It is about how deeply you can know each one — their strengths, their struggles, their passions, and their potential.

Vision

To be Johor's most trusted micro-school — where small is not a compromise but a philosophy

A school where neurodiverse learners thrive, where students create as much as they consume, and where every family feels like they belong to a community, not a institution.

Founder & Principal

Dr. Sean Ng

Founder & Principal

Dr. Sean Ng holds a doctorate and has over a decade of experience in education. He founded Edusky with a simple conviction: that education should see the whole child — academically, emotionally, and socially.

Under his leadership, Edusky has grown from a single classroom into a multi-service education provider, offering primary schooling, language training, corporate programmes, and community initiatives across Johor Bahru.

His vision for Edusky is an education group where every arm — school, language centre, training, enrichment, community — feeds into every other, creating a complete ecosystem for lifelong learning.
