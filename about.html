Founder & Director

Dr. Ng Peng Long

Founder & Director

Dr. Ng Peng Long is the Founder and Director of Edusky Private School. He holds a PhD in Marketing from Universiti Sains Malaysia (USM) and brings with him over two decades of senior leadership and management experience in the tertiary education sector. Throughout his distinguished career, he has played a pivotal role in shaping academic institutions, developing student-centric programs, and driving educational excellence with a strong emphasis on holistic student development.

With extensive expertise in student engagement, academic pathway planning, and career development, Dr. Ng has guided countless students in making informed decisions about their educational journeys. His deep understanding of student behaviour, learning psychology, and emotional well-being enables him to support students not only academically, but also personally in helping them build confidence, resilience, and clarity in an increasingly competitive global environment.

Dr. Ng founded Edusky Private School in 2014 with a clear and unwavering conviction: every child is unique, and every child has the ability to learn effectively when placed in a comfortable, supportive, and well-structured environment. This philosophy continues to shape the school's direction, ensuring that each student receives personalised attention, targeted learning support, and the encouragement needed to reach their full potential.

Under his leadership, Edusky has grown into a trusted institution known for its nurturing environment, small class approach, and commitment to developing well-rounded individuals. Dr. Ng's vision extends beyond academic achievement. He is dedicated to cultivating students who are confident, adaptable, and equipped with the skills necessary to thrive in both their studies and future careers.

Today, Dr. Ng continues to lead Edusky with the same passion and dedication that inspired its founding more than a decade ago, providing parents with the assurance that their children are learning in an environment built on experience, integrity, care, and a genuine commitment to every child's success.

Customer Charter

We pledge, with full commitment, to deliver high-quality education to every family who entrusts their child to us. This charter is binding, public, and aligned with our vision and mission.

  1. 1.Delivering Quality Education — Teaching and learning that is effective, engaging, and student-centred. Every lesson, every worksheet, every conversation is directed toward the holistic development of academic ability and character.
  2. 2.Safeguarding Student Welfare — Maintaining a secure, clean, and conducive environment for the physical and emotional wellbeing of every pupil. Safety is not a policy; it is a condition of learning.
  3. 3.Serving with Warmth and Professionalism — Extending courteous, efficient, and attentive service to parents, guardians, and all who walk through our doors. Every phone call answered. Every concern acknowledged. No one is invisible.
  4. 4.Practising Effective Communication — Clear, accurate, and timely information on pupil progress and school activities through channels that reach you, not channels that merely discharge our duty.
  5. 5.Nurturing Character and Noble Values — Educating pupils toward discipline, responsibility, and respect for teachers, peers, and community. We do not only teach subjects; we form human beings.
  6. 6.Acting Fairly and Equitably — Fair treatment without regard to background, ability, or individual difference. A child's starting point does not determine their standing here.

This is our solemn promise — to raise pupils who are knowledgeable, virtuous, and capable of holding their own in the world.

Our Journey

From One Classroom to a Community

2014

Edusky Learning Academy founded. A single classroom with a conviction: every child deserves to be known.

2016

Expanded to full primary Standards 1–6. Small-class philosophy took root as enrolment grew.

2019

Campus facilities upgraded. Purpose-built spaces for science, computing, and the arts.

2022

Rebranded as Edusky Private School. SMIPS registration JDA1001. MOE-registered KSSR curriculum.

2025

Student performances and cultural celebrations expanded on stage. First Deepavali showcase, drama club debut, and Teachers Day video filmed by students.

2026

Micro-school model formalised. Community programmes, parent workshops, and year-round enrichment camps launched.
