Getting Here

Driving Directions

From JB City Centre / CIQ

Head east on Jalan Tun Abdul Razak. Take Jalan Adda Utama into Taman Adda Height. Turn left at Jalan Adda 3/1. Edusky is on your right, No. 71. ~15 minutes.

From Aeon Mall Dato' Onn / EDL Highway

Exit at Bandar Dato' Onn interchange. Head toward Taman Adda Height via Jalan Adda Utama. Turn right at Jalan Adda 3/1. ~8 minutes.

From Mount Austin / Tebrau

Head west on Jalan Mutiara Emas. Connect to Jalan Adda Utama. Turn right at Jalan Adda 3/1. ~10 minutes.

Nearby Landmarks

Adda Heights Central Park — 2 min · Aeon Mall Dato' Onn — 8 min · KPJ Bandar Dato' Onn — 5 min · Johor Premium Outlet — 20 min · Senai Airport — 25 min.

Logistics

Parking & Access

Parking

Street parking along Jalan Adda 3/1. Drop-off bay directly in front of the school gate. Please do not block neighbour driveways.

School Bus

Optional transport covering Taman Adda Height, Bandar Dato' Onn, and nearby estates. Routes to be finalised based on demand.

Accessibility

Single-storey campus with step-free access to all classrooms and facilities. Contact us in advance if mobility assistance is needed.
