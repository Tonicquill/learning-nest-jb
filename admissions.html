Questions parents ask

We follow the KSSR (Kurikulum Standard Sekolah Rendah), Malaysia's national primary curriculum approved by the Ministry of Education. Our SMIPS registration code is JDA1001. Medium of instruction is Bahasa Malaysia with English.

We maintain a maximum teacher-student ratio of approximately 1:15. This is core to our philosophy — every child is known individually, taught personally, and supported fully.
Yes. We are an intentionally small school, which means teachers have the time and space to understand each child individually. Children who learn at a different pace, need extra patience, or benefit from a calmer environment often thrive here. Our small class sizes let us differentiate instruction naturally — not as a special programme, but as standard practice.
