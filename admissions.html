Admissions Process

Your journey to Edusky — step by step

No hidden steps. No surprise fees. Just a clear path from first message to first day.

1
💬

Discover

WhatsApp, call, or fill the inquiry form. Tell us your child's age and what you need.

You reach out → We reply within 24h
2
📍

Visit

Tour the campus. Meet Dr. Ng. See classrooms, labs, and the small-class magic.

You visit → We show you everything
3
📅

Trial Week

Your child joins real classes for a week. No commitment. Just experience.

You observe → We assess fit together
4
📝

Apply

Submit birth cert, IC, photos, previous reports. We send the full checklist.

You submit docs → We verify and guide
5
💳

Enrol

Sign the registration form. Pay the fee. Your child is officially an Edusky student.

You confirm → We welcome your family
6
🌟

Start

Uniform fitting, orientation day, first week check-in. We do not drop you after signup.

You begin → We stay close for 30 days

Most families complete steps 1–3 within one week. Steps 4–6 within two weeks.

We maintain a maximum teacher-student ratio of approximately 1:15. This is core to our philosophy — every child is known individually, taught personally, and supported fully.
