Admissions Process

How to enrol

Simple, transparent, personal. We want to meet you, not process you.

1. Get in Touch

WhatsApp us, call, or fill in the inquiry form below. Tell us your child's age, current school (if any), and what you're looking for.

2. Visit Us

Come to the school. Meet Dr. Sean or a senior teacher. Tour the classrooms, labs, and facilities. See what small feels like in person.

3. Trial Week (Optional)

Your child attends real classes for a week before you commit. They experience the environment, make friends, and you see if it fits. No pressure.

4. Submit Documents

Birth certificate, IC (if applicable), previous school reports, immunisation record, passport-sized photos. We'll give you the full checklist.

5. Registration & Payment

Complete the registration form, pay the registration fee, and your child is enrolled. Welcome to Edusky.
