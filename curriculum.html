Malaysian National Curriculum (KSSR)

Edusky follows the Kurikulum Standard Sekolah Rendah (KSSR) — Malaysia's national primary school curriculum, approved by the Ministry of Education (MOE). Our SMIPS registration code is JDA1001.

The KSSR curriculum emphasises knowledge, skills, and values across six pillars: Communication, Spiritual Attitude & Values, Humanities, Personal Development, Physical & Aesthetic Development, and Science & Technology.

Medium of instruction: English-led, Bahasa Malaysia where required. For subjects where the Ministry allows a language choice — Mathematics, Science, and others — we teach in English. Bahasa Malaysia is used for subjects where the curriculum mandates it.
