Since 2014

Over a decade of knowing children

Edusky was founded in 2014 by Dr. Ng Peng Long — not as a franchise, not as a venture, but as a conviction. One classroom, one teacher, one promise: that every child deserves to be known personally.

Ten years later, that conviction has grown into a full primary school, a language centre, a tuition hub, and a community of families who still get WhatsApp replies within the hour. Longevity matters in education. It means we have seen children grow from Standard 1 to secondary school. It means our systems are tested, our teachers are experienced, and our reputation is earned — not bought.
