Better One's Life

Clubs

Discover Your Passion

Clubs run during lunch and after school. No extra fees for most activities.

Taekwondo

Builds discipline, fitness, and self-confidence through martial arts. Students learn respect, perseverance, and self-defence skills that serve them beyond the dojang. Open to all standards.

Art & Performances

Develops creativity, self-expression, and presentation confidence through visual arts, drama, and mixed-media projects. Students exhibit and perform at term-end showcases, building pride in their work.

Red Crescent Society

Nurtures empathy, responsibility, and practical life skills through first aid training and community service. Students learn basic medical response and volunteer for local causes, becoming caring citizens.

Design Technology

Strengthens critical thinking, innovation, and practical problem-solving through hands-on design projects and basic woodworking. Students build functional items and see ideas become reality.

Culinary Arts

Builds independence, nutrition awareness, and teamwork through cooking and baking. Students prepare simple dishes, understand food science, and gain life skills that last beyond the kitchen.

Field Trip

Sparks curiosity and deepens understanding through off-campus experiential learning at museums, nature parks, factories, and cultural sites. Classroom lessons come alive in the real world.
