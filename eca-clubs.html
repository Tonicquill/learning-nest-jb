Builder Team Briefing

This site is currently a comprehensive planning draft. Review the sections below before proceeding.

Plans: Club catalogue, weekly schedule, student council section.
Already have: Club cards, schedule table, council section structure.
Gaps: Coding club not public yet. Eco Warriors need volunteer lead. Council not formalised.
To fill: Recruit volunteer club leads. Confirm room and equipment. Formalise council in Term 2 2026.
Why this matters: ECA pages are decision-drivers for parents choosing between schools. "Does my child get to do more than worksheets?" Clubs show breadth and care. The Media Club is the content engine — students learn photography, editing, and storytelling while producing authentic school marketing material. It is a genuine educational win-win: real-world skills plus reduced content burden. The "propose a club" CTA invites parent investment and community ownership.
Watch out for: Do not promise clubs that lack committed leads. A cancelled club disappoints children and angers parents. STEM and Nature clubs are marked correctly as pilot/proposed — keep those labels until confirmed. The student council must be real, not ceremonial. A fake council undermines student trust.
How hero videos work: This page shows real Edusky footage in the hero — the "Homeschool Registration 2021" video. For every other page, the hero uses a placeholder stock video. The system is interchangeable: swap the MP4 filename in the HTML, or even use a still image, and the scroll effect works identically. Capture short clips of students running, laughing, presenting — drop them in, done.

Clubs

Discover Your Passion

Clubs run during lunch, after school, and on select Saturdays. No extra fees for most activities.

Wednesdays · 2:00PM

Taekwondo

Martial arts training building discipline, fitness, and self-defence skills. Open to all standards. Emphasis on respect and perseverance.

Fridays · 2:00PM

Art & Performances

Visual arts, drama, and mixed-media projects. Students exhibit and perform at term-end showcases and community events.

View Gallery

Thursdays · 2:00PM

Red Crescent Society

First aid skills, community service, and humanitarian values. Students learn basic medical response and volunteer for local causes.

Tuesdays · 2:00PM

Design Technology

Hands-on design projects, basic woodworking, and creative problem-solving. Students build functional items and develop practical skills.

Mondays · 2:00PM

Culinary Arts

Cooking and baking skills with emphasis on nutrition, hygiene, and teamwork. Students prepare simple dishes and understand food science.

Select Saturdays

Field Trip

Off-campus experiential learning — museums, nature parks, factories, and cultural sites. Brings classroom lessons into the real world.

Leadership

Student Council

Edusky students learn to lead early. The student council plans events, voices concerns, and represents their peers.

🏅

Council Roles

President, Vice President, Secretary, and Class Representatives elected each term. Campaigns are short, kind, and focused on ideas.

💡

What They Do

Organise spirit days, suggest canteen improvements, lead assembly presentations, and help welcome new students.
