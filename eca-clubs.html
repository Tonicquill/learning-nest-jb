NOT FOR PUBLIC — PLANNING DRAFT

Builder Team Briefing

This site is currently a comprehensive planning draft. Review the sections below before proceeding.

Plans: Club catalogue, weekly schedule, student council section.
Already have: Club cards, schedule table, council section structure.
Gaps: Coding club not public yet. Eco Warriors need volunteer lead. Council not formalised.
To fill: Recruit volunteer club leads. Confirm room and equipment. Formalise council in Term 2 2026.
Why this matters: ECA pages are decision-drivers for parents choosing between schools. "Does my child get to do more than worksheets?" Clubs show breadth and care. The Media Club is the content engine — students learn photography, editing, and storytelling while producing authentic school marketing material. It is a genuine educational win-win: real-world skills plus reduced content burden. The "propose a club" CTA invites parent investment and community ownership.
Watch out for: Do not promise clubs that lack committed leads. A cancelled club disappoints children and angers parents. STEM and Nature clubs are marked correctly as pilot/proposed — keep those labels until confirmed. The student council must be real, not ceremonial. A fake council undermines student trust.
