Builder Team Briefing

This site is currently a comprehensive planning draft. Review the sections below before proceeding.

Plans: Calendar of upcoming and past events — open days, camps, workshops, concerts.
Already have: Open Day cards, camp cards, workshop cards, past archive structure.
Gaps: Dates are preliminary. No live registration. Past archive empty. No confirmed speakers.
To fill: Confirm Q3/Q4 event dates with Dr. Sean. Set up WhatsApp RSVP or Eventbrite. Book speakers.
Why this matters: Events are lead magnets. Open days convert browsers into visitors. Holiday camps generate non-enrolment revenue and expose non-Edusky families to the brand. The calendar creates urgency: "next open day is 12 July — I need to decide by then." Each event is a new page Google can index.
Watch out for: Announcing events without confirmed dates = broken trust if cancelled. Empty past-archive makes us look like we never do anything. Overpromising speakers who bail = disappointment.
How hero videos work: The pinned video hero uses a placeholder stock video. Drop any MP4 or image into the videos/ folder, update the filename in the HTML, and the scroll effect works automatically. No coding needed. The ECA page already uses real Edusky footage as a demo.
