Builder Team Briefing

This site is currently a comprehensive planning draft. Review the sections below before proceeding.

Plans: Masonry grid of curated images across 6 categories, filter tabs, lightbox.
Already have: ~60 curated images, lightbox JS, CSS masonry, category filters.
Gaps: Some images are low-res Facebook thumbnails. Missing captions. Not all 700+ images included.
To fill: Replace thumbnail-only images with full-res originals. Write captions per image. Expand categories.
Why this matters: Visual proof beats any copy. 60 real images of smiling children and actual classrooms remove doubt faster than a thousand words. Parents filter by category to find what THEY care about: a sports parent checks "Outdoor/Sports," an arts parent checks "Celebrations." It is self-service persuasion.
Watch out for: Low-res thumbnails make us look cheap. Images without media consent are a legal risk. A gallery full of stock photos kills authenticity — every photo must be ours.
