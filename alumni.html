NOT FOR PUBLIC — PLANNING DRAFT

Builder Team Briefing

This site is currently a comprehensive planning draft. Review the sections below before proceeding.

Plans: Alumni profiles, "Where Are They Now" stories, achievement wall, story submission form.
Already have: Profile structure, form prototype, trophy grid.
Gaps: No alumni data. First graduating cohort is years away (Std 6 now).
To fill: Reach out to early leavers/transfer students as interim alumni. Add "Share Your Story" to WhatsApp community.
Why this matters: Alumni are the ultimate trust signal — proof that Edusky graduates become capable young people. Even a modest profile ("now at SMK XYZ, loves robotics") answers the parent question "what happens after Edusky?" The alumni form collects stories we can turn into blog content. Linking to Edusky Education Group shows parents there is a pipeline beyond primary.
Watch out for: Do not fabricate alumni. An empty alumni page is better than a fake one — parents will verify. Get consent before posting any student name or photo. Respect privacy of transfer students who may not want to be featured.
How hero videos work: The pinned video hero uses a placeholder stock video. Drop any MP4 or image into the videos/ folder, update the filename in the HTML, and the scroll effect works automatically. No coding needed. The ECA page already uses real Edusky footage as a demo.
