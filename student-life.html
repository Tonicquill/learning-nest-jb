Student Life

Every student carries a production studio in their pocket. We channel creativity into community, content, and confidence.

The Engine

Student Production System

We do not create content for students. We channel what they already do into something that benefits them — and the school.

🎥

Media Club

4–6 students. Roles: Editor, Photographer, Videographer, Writer. They shoot events, film activities, write blog posts, and produce social media content. One teacher advises. Students use their own phones. This club is the school's content team.

🤝

Community Service Club

The formal vehicle for all CSR and volunteer work. Students plan, organise, and execute service projects. Generates the highest-value marketing content — Edusky students doing things other schools' students do not do.

🎨

Creative Club

Art Club produces visible output: school posters, event flyers, website illustrations, reception displays. Debate Club enters inter-school competitions that put Edusky's name in front of other schools' parents.

Incentive System

SkyPoints

Earn rewards for creating, contributing, and representing Edusky. Points build skills, confidence, and recognition.

How Points Work

Published article / blog post20 pts
Photo or video used on social media10 pts
Content with 50+ engagement (bonus)15 pts
Club officer role (per semester)30 pts
External event representation25 pts
CSR volunteering20 pts
4-week contribution streak (bonus)25 pts

Reward Tiers

Tier 1 — 25–50 pts Extra recess, sit-anywhere day, first lunch pick
Tier 2 — 75–150 pts Star Contributor badge, free camp entry, lunch with Dr. Sean
Tier 3 — 200+ pts Edusky Creator certificate, RM 50–100 voucher, fee micro-scholarship
Tier 4 — Annual Top Ambassador title, printed personal portfolio, reference letter from principal

SkyPoints spreadsheet to be set up in Google Sheets. Leaderboard displayed in main hall.

Digital Responsibility

Phone Licence

Students who join the Media Club or content team earn a physical card granting phone use during designated content-creation time.

Requires completing a 10-minute digital responsibility briefing and signing a student media agreement. Can be revoked for misuse.

This reframes phones from distraction to tool — a creative device with accountability.

📱

Student Media Pass

Physical card design to be created. Badge photo + QR code linking to student portfolio.

Student Press

The Edusky Gazette

A primary school with a student-run newspaper is an anomaly in Johor Bahru. It signals culture, communication skills, and institutional seriousness.

4–8 pages per issue. Student news, opinion pieces, interviews, creative writing, puzzles. Produced in Canva and Google Docs.

PDF sent to all parents via WhatsApp Community

Posted on school website as blog entry

Printed copies in reception for visitors

Target: Issue 1 within 6 weeks

Community

Events & Workshops

CSR projects, parent workshops, expert talks, student volunteering — a mega-school runs events. A micro-school builds community.

🤝

CSR Volunteering

Partner with registered charities. Students run workshops for underprivileged communities: reading programs, basic computing, creative arts. Parents drive them there. The school coordinates.

First event to be planned after media consent forms signed. Target: within 3 months.

🎯

Expert Speaker Series

Pediatric psychologist, occupational therapist, or child psychiatrist speaks at Edusky. Topics: understanding ADHD, managing screen time, building emotional resilience. Open to the public.

First session to be scheduled. Film with speaker consent for YouTube content.

Parent Education Workshops

Parenting tips, education articles, school announcements. Delivered via WhatsApp Community and in-person sessions. Value-added content that builds trust before enrollment.
