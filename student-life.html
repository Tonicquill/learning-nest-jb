Better One's Life

The Engine

Student Production System secondary

We do not create content for students. We channel what they already do into something that benefits them — and the school.

🎥

Media Club secondary

4–6 students. Roles: Editor, Photographer, Videographer, Writer. Under teacher mentorship, they learn photography, editing, and storytelling by covering real school events. Students use their own phones. The content they produce builds a portfolio of real-world skills — and helps the school share authentic stories.

🤝

Community Service Club

The formal vehicle for all CSR and volunteer work. Students plan, organise, and execute service projects — building leadership, empathy, and organisational skills that transfer far beyond the classroom.

🎨

Creative Club

Art Club learns design thinking by creating school posters, event flyers, and exhibition pieces. Debate Club develops argumentation and confidence through inter-school competitions. Both build transferable creative and critical thinking skills.

Incentive System

SkyPoints secondary

Earn rewards for creating, contributing, and representing Edusky. Points build skills, confidence, and recognition.

How Points Work

Published article / blog post20 pts
Photo or video used on social media10 pts
Content with 50+ engagement (bonus)15 pts
Club officer role (per semester)30 pts
External event representation25 pts

Tier 3 — 200+ pts Edusky Creator certificate, RM 50–100 voucher, fee micro-scholarship

Digital Responsibility

Student Media Pass secondary

Students who join the Media Club or content team earn a physical card granting phone use during designated content-creation time.

Requires completing a 10-minute digital responsibility briefing and signing a student media agreement. Can be revoked for misuse.

This reframes phones from distraction to tool — a creative device with accountability.

Student Media Pass secondary

Personalised badge with photo and QR code linking to the student portfolio.

Leadership

Student Council

Edusky students learn to lead early. The council plans events, voices concerns, and represents their peers.

🏅

Council Roles

President, Vice President, Secretary, and Class Representatives elected each term. Campaigns are short, kind, and focused on ideas.

💡

What They Do

Organise spirit days, suggest canteen improvements, lead assembly presentations, and help welcome new students.

Elections held each term. All students are encouraged to participate.

Real World

Beyond the Classroom

Textbooks open doors. Experience builds bridges. We put students in rooms where real decisions are made.

🤝

CSR & Volunteering

Students run workshops for underprivileged communities: reading programs, basic computing, creative arts. Not simulation. Real service.

🌎

Expos & Booths

Edusky students represent the school at education fairs, community expos, and inter-school events. They pitch, present, and answer questions from strangers.

🎤

Expert Talks & Career Corners

Monthly sessions with professionals: engineers, doctors, artists, entrepreneurs. Students ask hard questions and see what real careers look like.

💻

Media & Content Creation

Students publish for real audiences — TikTok, Instagram, the school website. Metrics matter. Engagement is feedback. This is digital literacy with real-world impact.

💼

Student Enterprise

Small fundraising projects run by students: bake sales, craft markets, event ticketing. Budgeting, pricing, profit — economics by doing.

External visits and CSR partnerships open to all students. Details announced via WhatsApp.

Community

Events & Workshops

CSR projects, parent workshops, expert talks, student volunteering — a mega-school runs events. A micro-school builds community.

🤝

CSR Volunteering

Partner with registered charities. Students run workshops for underprivileged communities: reading programs, basic computing, creative arts. Parents drive them there. The school coordinates.

Events run throughout the year. Parents welcome to volunteer as drivers and mentors.

🎯

Expert Speaker Series

Pediatric psychologist, occupational therapist, or child psychiatrist speaks at Edusky. Topics: understanding your child's development, managing screen time, building emotional resilience. Open to the public.

Sessions scheduled each term. Open to the public. Recordings shared with parent permission.

👩‍🏫

Parent Education Workshops

Parenting tips, education articles, school announcements. Delivered via WhatsApp Community and in-person sessions. Value-added content that builds trust before enrollment.

👥

Alumni Network

"Where Are They Now" stories on social media and website. A testimonial from a 20-year-old who went through Edusky at 10 is more credible than any parent review.

Alumni stories published monthly. Former students welcome to reconnect anytime.
