2026 Enrollment Now Open

Published June 2026 · By Edusky Communications Team

Edusky Private School is now accepting applications for the 2026 academic year. Places are available across Standards 1 through 6, with small class sizes that ensure every child is seen, known, and nurtured.

What makes this enrollment cycle special is our continued commitment to the micro-school philosophy. We are not scaling up. We are doubling down on what works: personalised attention, strong parent communication, and a culture where children feel safe to be themselves.

What is New for 2026

  • Media Club officially launched — students now produce school content
  • SkyPoints incentive system live — reward positive participation
  • Holiday camps open to non-Edusky students for the first time
  • Trial week available before full enrollment commitment

We invite you to visit us in person. No appointment is required during school hours, though a quick WhatsApp heads-up helps us prepare. Come see what small feels like.
